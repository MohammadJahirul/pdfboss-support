# PDF Boss Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

PDF Boss is developed by Md Jahirul Islam. Contact [mohammadjahir609@gmail.com](mailto:mohammadjahir609@gmail.com) with privacy questions.

## Documents and processing

PDF Boss stores imported and created documents in its local app storage on your device. Reading, editing, scanning, on-device text recognition, and the optional supported-device page assistant use Apple frameworks on your device. PDF Boss does not require an account or operate a document cloud service. The app developer does not receive your PDFs or their contents through the app.

When you choose to import from Files or another provider, that provider's terms and privacy practices apply to the source file. When you choose to share or export a document, iOS sends it to the destination you select; that destination's privacy practices apply. Printing uses the printer or service you select.

## Data collected by the developer

PDF Boss does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising, tracking, or account sign-in. The developer does not collect personal data through the app. If you email support, the developer receives the email address, message, and any attachments you voluntarily send, solely to respond to your request. Support correspondence is handled through the email provider and may be retained as needed to resolve your request or comply with law. You can request deletion of support correspondence at the address above.

## Device permissions and storage

Camera access is used only when you choose to scan. Photos and Files access is used when you choose to import or export. Optional device authentication can protect access to the app on your device. App preferences and documents stay in app storage or in locations you explicitly choose through iOS. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored app data; keep copies of important PDFs.

## Children and changes

PDF Boss is a general-purpose document utility and does not knowingly collect children's personal data. If these practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date before the change takes effect.
