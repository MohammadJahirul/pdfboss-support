# PDF Boss Support

PDF Boss helps you read, scan, annotate, sign, organize, and export PDFs on iPhone and iPad.

For help, bug reports, or accessibility feedback, email [mohammadjahir609@gmail.com](mailto:mohammadjahir609@gmail.com). Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and the steps that caused the issue. Please do not email confidential PDFs unless you choose to share them for troubleshooting.

## Quick help

- **Import a PDF:** Open PDF Boss and choose Import, or share a PDF from Files to PDF Boss.
- **Mark up a page:** Open a PDF and use text markup, Draw, text boxes, signatures, sticky notes, or shapes.
- **Erase ink:** Choose the drawing eraser and move its tip across the part of a stroke you want to remove. Undo is available.
- **Erase original content:** Use the original-content tool to create a separate image-only copy. Inspect the exported copy before sharing. Your original remains in the library.
- **Recover a document:** Check Recently Deleted or a document's available previous version before deleting it permanently.

PDF Boss does not require an account. The optional page assistant is available only on supported devices with Apple Intelligence ready. For general questions, contact the email above.
